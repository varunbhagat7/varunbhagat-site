NOW · STANFORD RESEARCH DESIGN & INNOVATION (RDI)
Oceanic hydropower and sustainability
At the Naval Academy, I studied the engineering underlying hydroelectricity. At sea, I felt the ocean’s power firsthand. Now, through a Research, Design & Innovation (RDI) project at Stanford, I explore how ocean-based solutions can accelerate the energy transition, build a more sustainable future and deliver clean, abundant energy to power everyday communities and the AI buildout.
Tidal turbines
Underwater turbines spun by the predictable flow of tidal currents to generate steady power.
Wave energy converters
Devices that capture the up-and-down motion of surface waves and convert it into electricity.
Thermal energy cycles
Heat engines that run on the temperature gap between warm surface water and cold deep water.
Desalination
Techniques that remove salt from seawater, using membranes or heat, to produce fresh water.
